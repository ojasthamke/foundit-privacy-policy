📌 1. Platform Overview & Core Objective

Found It ("Found It", "FIT", "we", "us", or "our") is a specialized Hyperlocal Offline Store & Product Locator application designed and operated in India.

Our singular real-world objective is to assist shoppers in locating nearby physical, brick-and-mortar retail stores that carry specific everyday products (groceries, electronics, personal care, pharmaceuticals, hardware, stationery) in live stock offline.

💡 Our Privacy Principle: Offline Shopping By Design

Because Found It is an offline locator and not a delivery aggregator or courier marketplace, we deliberately do not collect home delivery addresses, apartment numbers, delivery instructions, or banking credentials. You browse what local shops have in stock, then walk in and purchase directly at the retailer's physical counter.

🔑 2. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

We request and utilize the minimum necessary device permissions required to compute geographic proximity to local brick-and-mortar stores:

📍

Precise Location (Foreground Only)

Purpose: We request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION strictly while the application is active in the foreground to calculate walking and driving distances to verified local physical shops.

⛔ We NEVER track background location when the app is closed.

📷

Camera & Storage (Merchant Portal Only)

Purpose: Used exclusively in the Business App (FA) by verified shop owners to upload real storefront photos and physical product barcodes. Customer app users are never asked for camera permissions.

🔔

System Notifications

Purpose: Used to notify users of critical local market opening updates, store operational statuses, or in-app service announcements. We never send unsolicited spam advertisements.

📱

Device & Diagnostic Telemetry

Purpose: Technical diagnostics such as operating system version, crash reports, and network connectivity status to maintain application stability and debug connection faults.

🚫 3. Zero Online Payments & Zero Delivery Statement

To maintain utmost data minimization and consumer protection, the Found It architecture enforces the following fundamental rules:

  • No Payment Card Industry (PCI) Data: We never ask for, collect, store, or process debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI MPINs.
  • No In-App Payment Escrow: All payments occur physically in-person between you and the shop owner using cash, card, or direct merchant QR at the shop's billing counter.
  • Zero Delivery Logistics: We do not operate delivery fleets, delivery boys, dispatchers, or doorstep shipping. We do not ask for delivery directions or landmark drop-off instructions.

⚙️ 4. How We Use Your Information

All information processed by Found It is used strictly to fulfill the core offline locator experience:

  1. Identify nearby physical retail stores in your selected operational city (e.g. Yavatmal) carrying your desired product in verified inventory.
  2. Calculate real-time walking and driving distance in kilometers.
  3. Provide one-tap physical directions, verified shop addresses, and direct phone/WhatsApp contact to physical store proprietors.
  4. Prevent fraudulent spam, automated bot abuse, or scraping of local catalogue data.

🌐 5. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to third-party data aggregators or advertising networks.

Data is transferred solely across encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 channels to verified infrastructure providers:

  • Database & Cloud Hosting: Managed Supabase Cloud infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies for catalogue indexing.
  • Geocoding & Map Display: OpenStreetMap / Nominatim API for non-commercial reverse geocoding to resolve street locality names.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by court subpoenas, warrants, or applicable Indian laws.

🔒 6. Security & Synthetic Data Prohibition

Security is engineered into every tier of our codebase:

  • TLS 1.3 Encryption: All mobile API requests communicate over modern cryptographic TLS connections.
  • Strict Database Row-Level Security: Customers cannot view or alter other users' preferences or merchant credentials.
  • Zero Synthetic Profiles: We strictly prohibit phantom customer profiles, simulated telephone numbers, or fake customer seed records in our live production environment.

🗑️ 7. Data Retention & Right to Erasure (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), you maintain complete ownership over your data:

  • Local Device Cache: You can clear recently searched items and cached city preferences directly in the app or via Android App Settings → Clear Storage.
  • Permanent Deletion / Erasure: To delete any profile, merchant listing, or user log associated with you, submit a deletion request to our Grievance Officer at privacy@foundit.org.in. All requests are permanently scrubbed within 48 business hours.

👶 8. Children's Privacy

Found It does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with identifying data, please inform our Grievance Officer immediately so we can expunge the data from our logs.

🔄 9. Policy Amendments

We may periodically update this policy to reflect product improvements or statutory changes. Any material amendments will be announced with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document and through an in-app notice.

📜 10. Terms of Service & User Agreement

1. Informational Locator Nature: Found It is solely an information discovery platform that helps shoppers find products physically stocked in local neighbourhood retail shops. Found It is not a seller, courier, delivery service, or financial intermediary.

2. In-Person Contracts: All sales contracts, payments, product warranties, exchanges, and physical handovers take place strictly and exclusively between the consumer and the respective independent offline merchant.

3. Shelf Availability & MRP: While Found It works diligently to reflect live in-store inventory and maximum retail prices (MRP) via real-time merchant updates, inventory is subject to rapid physical customer turnover. We recommend users utilize the in-app WhatsApp or Direct Call feature to confirm availability before traveling.

4. Governing Law: These terms and privacy policies are governed by the laws of India, subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Yavatmal, Maharashtra, India.
